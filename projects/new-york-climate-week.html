The Power of Climate Week NYC

Climate Week NYC stands as one of the world's most important global climate gatherings. It serves as a vital meeting point where international leaders, policymakers, industry innovators, scientists, and grassroots activists come together from diverse fields to accelerate climate action and exchange visionary solutions.

During Climate Week NYC 2026, Aguatura participated with a series of diverse events and community engagements, bridging the gap between high-level discourse and local, hands-on environmental impact.

Our Key Activities during Climate Week

1. The Movie Screening

We hosted a special screening of the powerful documentary film narrated by Benedict Cumberbatch, exploring humanity's profound connection with nature and inspiring a shift toward sustainable stewardship.

2. Community Restoration

Hands-on environmental action partnering with the local New York community around Manhattan to restore natural green spaces, enhance biodiversity, and protect urban ecosystems.

3. WaterWays Initiative

Engaging in WaterWays sessions—a local initiative uniting actors from science, art, and public sectors to promote deep water literacy and conservation awareness across New York.

4. Columbia University Pitch

Supporting local students at Columbia University as they pitched innovative, sustainable business ideas designed to tackle modern ecological and climate challenges.
