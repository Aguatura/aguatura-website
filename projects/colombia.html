Main Challenges

Colombia hosts nearly 10% of global biodiversity on less than 1% of the Earth's land surface. However, rapid land-use changes and environmental pressures threaten critical natural ecosystems across the country.

  • Accelerating deforestation and illegal logging threatening high-biodiversity biomes.
  • Vulnerability of high-altitude páramo ecosystems supplying water to major urban hubs.
  • Pressure from unsustainable agricultural expansion and illegal mining activities.
  • Fragmented resource mobilization between international funders and local communities.

Our Approach

Partner

Together with WWF Colombia, regional authorities, and local fincas, our approach focuses on connecting strategic conservation fundraising with hands-on fieldwork and community stewardship.

NGO Strategic Alignment

Connecting international non-profit fundraising in Bogotá directly with high-impact biodiversity field programs.

Páramo & Water Protection

Protecting high-altitude natural sponges in Chingaza National Park to safeguard critical water supplies and endemic species.

Sustainable Agroforestry

Integrating native stingless bee meliponiculture and organic shade-grown coffee practices in Salento.

Key steps of our conservation initiative

1. Mobilize

  • Align institutional funding models in Bogotá with key conservation priorities across regional offices.

2. Partner

  • Unite local communities, civil society, and regional government bodies through "Unidos por el Planeta".

3. Protect

  • Monitor vulnerable páramo ecosystems and establish strict protection zones for endangered flora and fauna.

4. Sustain

  • Promote fair-trade agroforestry, pollinator preservation, and eco-tourism livelihoods with local farmers.

How can you take action

Micro Level

  • Support Fair-Trade Agroforestry: Purchase directly from small-scale shade-grown coffee fincas and local artisans.
  • Promote Native Pollinators: Support stingless bee (meliponiculture) initiatives and urban biodiversity gardens.
  • Responsible Ecotourism: Choose certified community-guided visits when exploring delicate páramo ecosystems.
  • Personal Skill Sharing: Offer technical, design, or educational support to grassroots environmental monitors.

Meso Level

  • Connect Institutional Funders with Fieldwork: Facilitate direct funding channels between global donors and local NGOs.
  • Multi-Stakeholder Alliances: Join regional alliances like "Unidos por el Planeta" to align cross-sector climate goals.
  • Community Advocacy & Education: Organize educational workshops on páramo water protection and biodiversity.
