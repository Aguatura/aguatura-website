We met and made each other's passion our common passion

Marie and Johannes met for the first time in April 2022 while traveling. Since then, they have explored a wide variety of countries together, discovering the beauty and diversity of nature around the world. What connects them most is their shared passion for nature and social engagement.

Driven by this passion, they founded their NGO Aguatura—a way to turn their vision into reality and actively contribute to protecting the environment and supporting communities.

Marie

Co-Founder
"We founded Aguatura to unite people and knowledge, driven by the belief that protecting our planet is a shared responsibility."

Academic Background

I studied business and economics, before completing a Master's in Sustainability for Business Transformation and a second Master's in European Governance and Political Science.

This highlighted the gap between theory and reality and the importance of exploring how these challenges are actually addressed on the ground.

Professional Experience

Professionally, I spent over four years working between Paris and Berlin in the energy sector as a Sustainability and Digital Analyst, gaining insight into Europe's transition toward more sustainable systems.

This role allowed me to better understand how these shifts impact systems and households, highlighting the crucial role of innovation.

Social Engagement

Alongside my studies and career, I have built extensive international NGO experience (e.g., Saint Vincent de Paul in Normandy, Bridges Today in Peru, and WWF in Colombia).

Curious and committed, I continue to explore how social action and environmental protection can come together to create meaningful change.

Johannes

Co-Founder
"We believe we should see ourselves as part of nature rather than apart from it, working together to preserve its balance and beauty. Aguatura stands for this."

Academic Background

I began studying Business and Economics for my bachelor’s degree in 2017 at the University of Passau. During a seminar, I discovered Corporate Social Responsibility, which became a turning point.

For my master’s degree, I decided to look at business through a "green" lens, equipping me with the skills to help transform companies into drivers of a sustainable future.

Professional Experience

For over three years, I worked in the field of climate change and sustainability across several consulting companies, helping organizations on their journey toward sustainability.

These experiences showed me how companies can tackle sustainability challenges and that organizations are not just part of the problem—they are also a crucial part of the solution.

Social Engagement

Over the years, I have engaged in various volunteer projects related to social and environmental topics. Being part of such environments has always been inspiring.

These experiences ultimately motivated me to co-found Aguatura, as a way to continue working toward causes I care about and to create meaningful change together.
