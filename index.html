Learning from local best practices to inspire action everywhere

Around the world, countless local initiatives successfully protect nature. Yet, these valuable strategies often remain hidden from the broader public.

We bridge this gap by giving local approaches greater visibility—inspiring engagement for nature and people.

Our Core Focus Areas

Water Management

Advancing sustainable water resource conservation, clean supply, and watershed protection to secure our most vital resource.

Biodiversity

Preserving natural habitats and protecting endangered species through targeted ecological research and field action.

Our Approach

We work closely with selected NGOs and local changemakers from different cultures and regions to collect a rich variety of best practices. Through personal field trips, interviews, and hands-on research, we bring these inspiring local strategies together in one place.
