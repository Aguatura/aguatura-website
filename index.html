Learning from local best practices to inspire action everywhere

Our modern world is currently facing a series of critical environmental and social challenges that deeply threaten the delicate balance of life as we know it.

Yet, in every corner of the world, countless inspiring local initiatives are successfully protecting nature, restoring ecosystems, and building lasting resilience.

We actively bridge this gap by giving these powerful local approaches greater global visibility, thereby inspiring meaningful engagement for both nature and people.

Our Core Focus Areas

Water Management

Focusing on river and marine protection alongside sustainable water use that benefits both nature and human communities.

Biodiversity

Preserving natural habitats, enhancing biodiversity, and promoting sustainable agriculture and greener cities.

Our Approach

We work closely with selected NGOs and local changemakers from different cultures and regions to collect a rich variety of best practices.

Through personal field trips, interviews, and hands-on research, we bring these inspiring local strategies together in one place.

With Aguatura, we aim to create a knowledge exchange platform where interested people can find inspiration to take positive action in their own communities.
